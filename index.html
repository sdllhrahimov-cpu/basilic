Our Story

Basilic was conceived as an unhurried sanctuary in the energetic heart of Tashkent. Drawing inspiration from Mediterranean coastal villas, Parisian culinary discipline, and high-modern architectural restraint, our space provides a calm refuge for guests who appreciate exacting craft.

From our custom charcoal Josper ovens to temperature-regulated wine cellars, every detail is engineered to let ingredients express their highest natural state without compromise.
